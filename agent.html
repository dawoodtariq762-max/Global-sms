How to find your Binance UID

Warning: Payments are sent to the Binance UID you save here. Please double-check your UID before saving — entering an incorrect UID can result in funds being sent to the wrong account. You are responsible for the accuracy of your Binance UID.
Step 1 — Open the Binance app
Log in to the Binance app on your phone.
Step 2 — Go to your Profile
Tap the profile icon (top left on the home screen).
Step 3 — Find your UID
Your Binance UID is shown on the profile page, right under your name (a number of 8–12 digits).
Step 4 — Copy and paste it here
Copy the full number and paste it into the Binance UID field, then save.
Using the Binance website instead
Log in at binance.com → hover over your profile icon (top right) → your Binance UID is displayed in the dropdown menu under your email. Copy that number and paste it here.
